What If Thinkers Uploader

What If Thinkers Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the What If Thinkers YouTube channel. It turns the owner's own video ideas into animated, hand-drawn style explainer videos and uploads them to the owner's channel.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads finished videos to the channel the owner signs in with, as private or scheduled videos.
  • Sets each video's custom thumbnail.
  • Uploads each video's English subtitle file.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.
